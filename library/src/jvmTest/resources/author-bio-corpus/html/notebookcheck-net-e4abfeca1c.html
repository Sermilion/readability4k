Google Pixel 11 Pro - Reviews and Specs

The Google Pixel 11 Pro earns favorable reviews for its upgraded cameras, brighter display, improved battery life, cooler operation, and expanding Gemini features. However, its higher price, weak gaming performance, incremental hardware changes, and unfinished software diminish its value.

Cons

The Google Pixel 11 Pro is a compact Android flagship with an aggregate review score of about 80 percent. Reviewers praise its upgraded 50 MP and 48 MP camera system, improved telephoto photography, and faster low-light capture. Its bright 6.3-inch 120 Hz OLED display, better battery life, cooler thermals, and faster connectivity enhance everyday use. Gemini integration introduces useful AI tools, although several features remain unnecessary, unreliable, or unfinished. The Tensor G6 delivers smooth general performance but trails competing flagship processors in gaming and benchmarks. Overall, the phone suits camera-focused users upgrading from older Pixels, while its higher price and modest generational gains make it less compelling for recent Pixel owners.
