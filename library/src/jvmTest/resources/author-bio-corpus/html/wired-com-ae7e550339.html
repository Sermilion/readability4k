The Best Prime Day Soundbar Deals

A soundbar is the easiest way to upgrade your home entertainment setup. Grab one of our favorites on sale during Prime Day.
Illustration: Isadora-Ayesa Lima; Courtesy of Amazon

Amazon Prime Day is here, and I found the best Prime Day soundbar deals to upgrade your TV audio. Some are close to 40 percent off, but you'll need an Amazon Prime membership to access many of the best deals.

I've covered all sorts of AV and TV gear for WIRED, including guides to the best soundbars, best art TVs, best RGB TVs, and the best home theater projectors. A soundbar remains one of the best ways to instantly improve your home entertainment setup. The right audio setup transforms movies—bullets in Top Gun: Maverick ricochet around the room, planes swoop down from the ceiling, and explosions shake couch cushions.

If you're still using your TV speakers, I implore you to grab on of these soundbars on sale. And be sure to check out our other sale coverage of the Absolute Best Prime Day Deals, the Best Prime Day Tech Deals, the Best Prime Day Headphone Deals, and more.
