Training stats and long battery life matter a lot when you want a smartwatch that can keep up with workouts and daily wear without costing a fortune. The Amazfit Active Max fits that kind of buyer well, and there’s a solid Amazon deal on it right now.

Amazon has the Amazfit Active Max for $139.99, down from its $169.99 recommended retail price. That’s an 18% discount off the RRP, which knocks $30 off the usual price. It’s not the lowest price we’ve seen this year, but it is still a nice drop for a newer fitness-focused smartwatch. It also has a 4.5-star review rating, which should give shoppers a bit more confidence.

The Amazfit Active Max is built around a 1.5-inch AMOLED display and an aluminum-alloy case, giving it a more polished look than many budget-friendly fitness watches. It also has 5 ATM water resistance, so it’s ready for swims, sweaty workouts, and everyday wear. One of the biggest draws here is battery life, with around 10 days under heavy use and up to roughly 25 days with lighter use.

For people who care more about training tools than flashy extras, the feature list is the real selling point. The watch includes GPS, offline maps, workout guidance, and broad health and fitness tracking. Recent updates have also added more training-focused tools, including HYROX features, rowing and pool-swim refinements, map management upgrades, and Zepp OS 6 improvements.

If you want a larger fitness smartwatch that covers the basics and then some, this Amazfit Active Max deal is worth a look at Amazon.
