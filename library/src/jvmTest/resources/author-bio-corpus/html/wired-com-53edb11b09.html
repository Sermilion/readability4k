The Best Prime Day Deals on Fitness and Wellness Gear

Fitness tech is on sale for Amazon Prime Big Deal Days. Here are the discounts worth checking out, from smartwatches and fitness trackers to walking pads and massage guns.
Courtesy of Amazon

Amazon Prime Day has no shortage of fitness tech and wellness gear on sale. Obviously, savvy bargain hunters know that not every discount makes for a good deal. Fortunately, I’ve sifted through hundreds of deals and rounded up the best discounts on products WIRED staffers have tested and vetted.

I will be updating this list daily with the freshest Prime Day fitness deals, so be sure to check back often. For more information on sales, browse our roundup of the Absolute Best Prime Day Deals.

For Samsung users who want an even cheaper smartwatch, the Watch7 is another option if you don’t need the latest health-tracking features. This Smart Walking Pad Is $93 Off

Photograph: Adrienne So

Urevo

CyberPad for Home

$480 $368 (23% off)

Amazon

$500

Urevo

The Urevo CyberPad is our top choice for a walking pad, primarily because of its 14-percent incline—the highest we’ve seen in our testing. I’ve seen the price drop even further, but $387 is a solid price for a top-tier walking pad now rather than waiting for Black Friday.

According to WIRED reviewer Kristin Canning, setup is a breeze, and you can easily control it via its remote or app. She appreciates the sturdy build and comfortable belt, which together ensure a solid workout experience. With a maximum speed of 4 mph and an adjustable incline that reaches either 9 or 14 percent, depending on how you position it, it offers enough versatility for walking. While it may not be the quietest option, it’s not disruptive if you're working or reading. Weighing in at 71 pounds, it provides stability, but it might not be the easiest to move around; the included wheels help with portability, though.
