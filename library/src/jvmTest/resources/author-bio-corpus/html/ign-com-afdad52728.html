The Studio Pro offers better audio quality than any other Beats headphone alongside excellent active noise cancelation. It carries over a lot of Apple's trademark features like spatial audio with head tracking, one touch pairing, and "Find My" functionality. It also has a Transparency mode so you can listen to the environment around you.

The Studio Pro also works well for Android smartphones. That's because it supports Android's most practical features like Google Fast Pair, Google Audio Switch, and Google Find My Device. Beats also has a mobile app for Android.

In wireless Bluetooth mode, the Studio Pro can last up to 40 hours on a single charge. The built in USB Type-C port supports fast charging but it can also be used as the connection method to your phone in wired mode, which is uncommon nowadays. Most other headphones require a 3.5mm output (which you can also do with the Studio Pro). The headphones fold in compactly and a soft carrying case is included for easy transport.
