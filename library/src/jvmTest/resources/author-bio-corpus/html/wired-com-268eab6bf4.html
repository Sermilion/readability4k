The Best Prime Day Deals on Our Favorite Toys and Games

Snag some gifts for your kiddos or unleash your inner child with the best Prime Day toy deals, including Lego, STEM toys, and board games.
Illustration: Isadora-Ayesa Lima; Courtesy of Amazon

Whether you need a birthday gift, you're looking ahead to the holidays, or your little ones simply deserve a treat, these are the best Prime Day toy deals this year.

I've been buying too many toys, board games, and Lego sets for my kids for many, many years. I'm not afraid to admit I still love playing with toys either. In fact, a lot of Lego sets are now aimed at adults who grew up with it (and have the disposable income). Everything here has been built, played with, and thoroughly tested by my family, and most have been tried out in a classroom full of kids (my wife is a teacher).

The tough, translucent Magna-Tiles click together easily to form two-dimensional shapes and three-dimensional objects. The classic set is a fantastic place to start, but be warned: There are themed sets, and you will end up buying more!

This immersive strategy game is all about gathering and trading resources to build roads, settlements, and cities on a randomly generated board of hexagonal tiles. The game is perfect for three or four players and usually lasts around an hour.

In Azul, you must make mosaics by matching tiles with different patterns and colors to get the maximum possible score. Azul is easy to grasp but supports many different tactical approaches and has surprising depth, making it one of the best family board games. A firm WIRED favorite, Ticket to Ride features steam engine styling and rules that are easy to grasp. All you must do is claim railway routes across the US and Canada using your colorful plastic rail cars. Spend cards to claim routes, rack up bonus points for connecting specific destinations, and the person with the highest score at the end wins. All aboard, choo, choo!

The Chameleon

Target

We regularly play this family favorite with visitors because it’s so fun, works for up to eight players, and is very easy to grasp. Each round is based on a single word from a category card determined by a dice roll, and while everyone else knows the word, the chameleon has to bluff their way through. Every player says something associated with the word for that round, and the guesser has to pick out the chameleon.
